The problem

Agents can act faster than companies can audit them.

Existing agent stacks focus on capability. AKSI focuses on accountability: what was authorized, what happened, what evidence was captured and what decision was made.

The asset

Runtime + trust protocol

AKSI combines agent execution, browser tooling, evidence collection, policy gating, cryptographic integrity and independent verification.

The deal

License, white-label or acquire.

The package is designed to be transferred as technology: source, history, protocol, verifier, deployment material and reproducible demonstrations.

Research frontier

Semantic Sampling

AKSI-S studies the minimum semantically meaningful evidence needed to reconstruct and verify an agent run. Open the research lab →

Important

Proof of execution ≠ proof of truth.

A receipt proves the recorded execution/decision and its integrity within the stated capture boundary. It does not magically make an AI claim true.

North star

Make every important AI action inspectable.

That is the product: not another model, but infrastructure around models and agents.
